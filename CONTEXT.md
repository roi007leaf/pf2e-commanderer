# Commander Tactics

This context describes how a Commander trains allies into a squad and signals them through tactics.

## Language

**Ally**:
A creature Pathfinder Second Edition regards as allied with the Commander. Alliance alone does not make a creature part of the Commander's squad.

**Squadmate**:
A willing ally instructed during daily preparations or a 10-minute drill and therefore able to respond to the Commander's tactics. The Commander also counts as a squadmate without consuming roster capacity.
_Avoid_: Nearby ally, aura member

**Squad Roster**:
The currently drilled non-Commander squadmates, limited by the Commander's Intelligence.
_Avoid_: Aura occupants, current targets

**Squad Candidate**:
An allied creature discovered as a possible addition to the Squad Roster. Candidate status has no rules effect until the creature is drilled as a Squadmate.
_Avoid_: Automatic squadmate

**Eligible Responder**:
A Squadmate who meets a tactic's current signal reach and other participation requirements.
_Avoid_: Ally, candidate

**Selected Responder**:
An Eligible Responder chosen for a tactic that signals fewer than all eligible Squadmates.
_Avoid_: Target

**Banner Aura**:
The Commander's active 30-foot emanation, or the 40-foot burst centered on a banner deployed with Plant Banner. It can constrain a tactic's signal reach or destination but never determines Squad Roster membership.

**Banner Origin**:
The Commander token while carried, or the persistent Scene point chosen when Plant Banner is used. Range and banner-dependent tactics measure from this origin.
